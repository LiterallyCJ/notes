### Kernel and Image as Subspaces

If $L: V \to W$,
$$
\begin{gather*}
ker(L) \text{ is a subspace of } V \\
im(L) \text{ is a subspace of } W
\end{gather*}
$$

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector space over a field $F$ and let $L: V \to W$ be a linear transformation. Let $x_1, \dots, x_n \in V$. Then:
* If $x_1, \dots, x_n \in V$ span $V$, then $L(x_1), \dots, L(x_n)$ span $im(L)$
* If $L(x_1), \dots, L(x_n)$ are linearly independent, then $x_1, \dots, x_n$ are linearly independent
### The Kernel Criterion

A linear transformation $L : V \to W$ is injective iff:
$$
ker(L) = \{0_V\}
$$
> Basically meaning the only vector that gets sent to 0 is the zero vector.
