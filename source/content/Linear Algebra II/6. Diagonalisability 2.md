### Diagonalisability

Let $F, V$ and $L: V \to V$. We say $L$ is diagonalisable if there exists a basis $x_1, \dots, x_n$ of $V$ such that the matrix $D$ representing $L$ with respect to this basis is a diagonal matrix.

Let $F$ be a field. We say the square matrix $A \in M_{n \times n}$ is diagonalisable if the linear transformation $L_A: F^n \to F^n, x \to Ax$ is diagonalisable.
