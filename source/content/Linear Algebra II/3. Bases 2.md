### Dimension

Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $F$. Suppose $x_1, \dots, x_n$ and $y_1, \dots, y_m$, then $m = n$.

Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $F$. If the vectors $x_1, \dots, x_n$ form a basis of $V$, we say that $V$ is of finite dimension and we call $N$ the dimension of $V$. We write $dim_F(V)$ or just $dim(V)$ for $n$.

> Basically bases are unique to a vector space, so if there are two bases, they are the same.

> The dimension of a vector space is the number of vectors in the basis.
